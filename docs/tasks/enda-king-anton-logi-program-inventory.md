  torsion, Ankle DF/PF, Rear/Fore-foot inversion/eversion, 1st MTP.
- **Strength (0–5, R/L, pain flag):** Obliques, Hip Flexion, Deep Rotators, Hip Abd (act vs pas), Hip
  Abduction, Hip Abduction/ER, Hip Adduction, Hip Adduction Magnus, Hip Ext (SL/LL), IRQ, IRH prone,
  Peroneals, Tib Post, FHL, Shoulder cuff (IR ext/int rotn).
- **Global movement:** Standing posture, Whole-body rotation, DL squat, SL squat, DL/SL calf raise.

**Anton's findings (the "why" behind his program):** left-sided hip/groin weakness — Hip Abduction 4/3,
Hip Abduction/ER 4/3, Hip Adduction Magnus 4/3, Deep Rotators 5/4, Hip Ext 5/4 — several **provoking groin
pain**; reduced left Hip IR (15R/10L), weaker left rec fem (Thomas), reduced FABER + tibial torsion left;
**SL squat: mild knee valgus RIGHT, moderate valgus LEFT**; DL squat **hip-dominant with rib flare** (poor
trunk/intersegmental control); standing posture sway++; shoulder-cuff weakness (5/3). → a classic athletic-
groin / intersegmental-control + left-limb picture. This is exactly what the movement screen (OHSA/SLDJ) +
Total Player Analysis should surface, and what these exercises target.

## Demo videos — NOT ingested (Anton / King own them)

The 17 clips in `Anton L/Motor Control/` are **Anton Logi's / Enda King's personal videos — they are NOT
imported into MicroPulse**. They stay where they are, for Anton's own reference. The library stores only the
**exercise entry** (name, phase, target, dose, coaching cue) — nothing that belongs to anyone. Each entry
has an **optional** `video_url` that a club may later fill with its **own** re-recorded demo, or leave empty
with a text coaching cue. So the video question doesn't block anything: the transferable part is the
exercise list + doses + phase/target mapping, which is what seeds the library.

## How it plugs into MicroPulse (without the videos)

- **Seeds the `corrective_exercises` / rehab-track library** (the brief): the King exercises + doses + King's
  phase framework — Motor Control → intersegmental-control phase; Run-Mech → linear-running/plyo (Level 2);
  Strength → Level 1 — each entry with its **target** (from Anton's assessment) and a **text coaching cue**.
  The `video_url` is optional and starts empty; a club demo can be added later. **No King/Anton video files
  are stored.**
- **The `Initial Ax` becomes the MicroPulse assessment schema** — the R/L, 0–5, pain-flag structure is a
  ready-made data model for a rehab assessment record, feeding Total Player Analysis (athlete-axis
  weaknesses) and gating the program.
- **Anton's record:** his assessment + program + the load-review hamstring finding are one player story —
  put them on his player profile / a rehab record.
- **What's transferable vs owned:** exercise *names*, dose structure and King's phase framework are not
  protectable → fine to seed the library. The *video files* are personal content → not used. Athlete health
  data stays consent- and access-gated (like clinical reports).
