Scene 1

Scene 2
Isometric PAP Primer. This isn't the main work — it's a wake-up. A few hard isometric pushes switch the nervous system on, and then your jumps and sprints come out sharper. Before explosive/power work on sharpening days — a primer, then the real thing.

Scene 3
The isometric PAP primer wakes the system up before explosive work — it's never the main set. Do three sets of three three-second maximal pushes into an immovable bar, at near-maximal intent. Then — and this part is essential — rest three to six minutes. That wait is when the potentiation shows up. Now do your explosive set, a jump or plyometric, at maximal height and speed. One isometric set isn't enough; it's the volume of pushes that triggers the boost.

Scene 4
The one thing to remember. Hard isometric pushes, wait, then explode. It primes the jump, it isn't the jump.
