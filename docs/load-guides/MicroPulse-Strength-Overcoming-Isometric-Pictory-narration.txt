MicroPulse — Strength setup: Overcoming Isometric (Max Strength) — narration for Pictory
============================================================

Scene 1
Overcoming Isometric. This is a push — you drive into a bar that can't move, as hard and as fast as you can. It builds max strength and how fast you produce force, with almost no joint stress.

Scene 2
Overcoming Isometric. This is a push — you drive into a bar that can't move, as hard and as fast as you can. It builds max strength and how fast you produce force, with almost no joint stress. Max-strength and rate-of-force days; great when joints need a break from heavy bar work.

Scene 3
An overcoming isometric is a push. You press into a bar or set of pins that simply won't move, so there's no joint movement at all. Set it at a deep angle — say a quarter-to-ninety-degree squat — because the long muscle length is what drives the strength gain. Push as hard AND as fast as you can from the very first second; the intent matters more than the stopwatch. Hold the max push about five seconds, rest two to three minutes, and do four sets. It's joint-friendly but fully switches on the nervous system for strength and rate of force.

Scene 4
The one thing to remember. Push into something fixed — hard and fast, deep angle, five seconds, four sets.
