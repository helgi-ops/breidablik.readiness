Scene 2
French Contrast. This is the most complete power method — four exercises done back-to-back as a single set, each one firing the muscle harder for the next. Used on a hard power day (around MD-3). It's the most demanding setup — big output, long rests.

Scene 3
Do the four exercises in a row as one set: a heavy lift, then a plyometric, then a light-loaded power move, then an assisted or accelerated jump. Only fifteen to thirty seconds between them — just enough to walk across. That short gap is the whole point: it makes the muscle fire harder on the next move. After the fourth exercise, rest three to five minutes fully, then repeat for three to five sets. Every rep stays explosive — if your speed drops, the set is done.

Scene 4
The one thing to remember. Four exercises, one set, short gaps, long rest. Keep every rep fast.
