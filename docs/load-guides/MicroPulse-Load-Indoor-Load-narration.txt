MicroPulse — Indoor Load Intelligence — narration (for Pictory)
========================================================

Scene 1
Indoor Load Intelligence. Real load indoors, without GPS.

Scene 2
Indoors, GPS does not work. Instead of showing zero — which looks exactly like a player who did nothing — MicroPulse deliberately discards the GPS signals and computes load from the inertial sensors.

Scene 3
Here's the Indoor Load Intelligence screen. Everything on it follows the same layered read: a plain verdict on top, the reason below, and the detail only if you open it.

Scene 4
How to read it. The explanation box at the top is part of the screen itself, not a manual: it states what is dropped, why, and what replaces it. An indoor score is not comparable with an outdoor one — the system says so rather than pretending it is.

Scene 5
When to open it. In a northern climate, where much of the year is played indoors, this is not an edge case but core business — open it for every indoor session. The research behind it: Brown et al. (2016) — unreliability of GPS indoors; Football Movement Profile. Requires Pro.

Scene 6
Indoor Load Intelligence — Real load indoors, without GPS. Read a decision, not a dataset; the detail is always one click away.
