MicroPulse — Strength setup: Cluster Sets — narration for Pictory
============================================================

Scene 1
Cluster Sets. Instead of grinding a full set, you break it with a short pause — so every rep stays fast and strong instead of fading at the end.

Scene 2
Cluster Sets. Instead of grinding a full set, you break it with a short pause — so every rep stays fast and strong instead of fading at the end. Strength / mechanical-stress days (MD-4 and development weeks).

Scene 3
Cluster sets break a hard set into mini-clusters. Take a moderately heavy load — around three sets of twelve at eighty percent of your one-rep-max — and put a fifteen-second pause partway through each set. That short pause lets you hold higher force and better quality for longer than if you ground the whole set out. It's your go-to when the day calls for a strength or mechanical-stress emphasis.

Scene 4
The one thing to remember. Same reps, one short pause — every rep stays strong, not grindy.
