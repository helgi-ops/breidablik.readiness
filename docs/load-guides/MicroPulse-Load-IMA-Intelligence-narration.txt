========================================================

Scene 1
IMA Intelligence. A biomechanical view of the day.

Scene 2
IMA Intelligence is a biomechanical view of a training day: stride distribution across eight bands, sprint strides against the player's own baseline, and left/right asymmetry in high-intensity changes of direction.

Scene 3
Here's the IMA Intelligence screen. Everything on it follows the same layered read: a plain verdict on top, the reason below, and the detail only if you open it.

Scene 4
How to read it. The weekly dial at the top shows where the squad sits against a typical week and projects where it lands if the current rate continues. Asymmetry over threshold is flagged — a persistent L/R difference is a known risk factor, not a one-day fluke.

Scene 5
When to open it. Useful on a Wednesday while there's still time to change the plan, and to watch asymmetry over time rather than one day at a time. The research behind it: Bishop et al. (2020) — L/R difference thresholds. Rests entirely on inertial data; requires Pro.
