Scene 3
Power contrast uses the same pairing, but light and fast. Do a light, explosive lift for three to five fast reps, then straight into a reactive jump for three to six reps. Rest two to four minutes and repeat. The weight is lighter than a strength contrast on purpose — you're chasing bar speed, not a grind. Keep the volume low and every rep sharp; stop the moment anything slows.

Scene 4
The one thing to remember. Light, fast, sharp. Speed on the bar beats weight on the bar here.
