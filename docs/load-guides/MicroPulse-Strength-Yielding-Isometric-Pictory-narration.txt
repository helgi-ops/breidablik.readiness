Scene 3
A yielding isometric is a hold. Instead of pushing into something fixed, you resist a load to keep a position. Sink to a long muscle length — a deep Spanish squat, or a long-lever bridge with the knee nearly straight — and hold against the load, controlled and sub-maximal, not an all-out effort. Hold thirty to forty-five seconds, rest about ninety seconds, and do three sets per exercise. It barely taxes the nervous system, which is why it fits a development day or the rebuild days just after a match.

Scene 4
The one thing to remember. Hold a long-length position against the load. Low fatigue, big for muscle and tendon.
