MicroPulse — Strength setup: Contrast (Strength + Plyo) — narration for Pictory
============================================================

Scene 1
Contrast training. You pair one heavy strength lift with one explosive, jumpy move — the heavy lift wakes the nervous system up so the explosive one is more powerful.

Scene 2
Contrast training. You pair one heavy strength lift with one explosive, jumpy move — the heavy lift wakes the nervous system up so the explosive one is more powerful. A staple strength-power day. Heavier and simpler than French Contrast.

Scene 3
Pick one heavy lift and one explosive move. Do the heavy lift for one to five hard reps, then go straight into the explosive one for three to six fast reps — no rest in between. That's the trick: the heavy lift primes your nervous system so the jump is more powerful. Rest two to five minutes after the pair, then repeat for three to six sets. Every explosive rep has to be maximal — if it slows down, rest longer or stop.

Scene 4
The one thing to remember. Heavy lift, then straight into an explosive one. The heavy primes the power.
