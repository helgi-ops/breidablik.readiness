MicroPulse — Overview / homepage promo — narration for Pictory
============================================================

Scene 1
Meet MicroPulse — an athlete-management platform that turns a flood of data into one clear decision per player, every day. Readiness, physical load, match performance and return-to-play — all explained, all on one page.

Scene 2
Modern sport drowns coaches in data: GPS, wellness, force plates, video, stats. But at eight in the morning, before training, nobody has time to open ten dashboards. The signal gets lost in the noise.

Scene 3
MicroPulse flips it. One page every morning gives you one verdict per player. The decision lives right there; the full depth is one click away when you want the why. It can look powerful, but it's really just one page — everything else is its detail.

Scene 4
At its heart is a simple traffic light. Every player is green, amber or red — but compared to his own normal, not a league average. A steady player and a jumpy one are judged against themselves, so the colour actually means something for him.

Scene 5
Instead of scanning twenty-five players, MicroPulse hands you the short list — who needs attention today, grouped and ranked, each with the reason. You spend your attention where it counts.

Scene 6
Under the readiness sits the physical layer. MicroPulse separates mechanical load — the pounding of braking, cutting and impacts — from metabolic load, the energy cost of high-speed running. That tells you whether a tired player needs less pounding or less running. And with IMA it works indoors too, where GPS goes blank.

Scene 7
It closes the loop to the match — movement, intensity by half, and each player's stats sitting right next to his physical load. Not just who's tired, but how the team actually played.

Scene 8
On the planning side, you build one strength session — the full ready-day version — and MicroPulse derives the reduced and the recovery versions automatically, so the work always matches the day, with a built-in auditor keeping it balanced.

Scene 9
When a player gets hurt, MicroPulse walks him up a clinical return-to-play ladder — force-plate testing and research-backed, criteria-based rehab for hamstrings, tendons and ankles — every stage cited, the clinician always in charge.

Scene 10
Here's what makes it different. Nothing is a black box. Every verdict shows its plain-language why, how confident it is, and the research behind it. Rules make the decision; the AI only explains it, and labels itself as AI. And any coach can override, with the reason logged.

Scene 11
The same verdict is visible everywhere. The head coach gets the answer in five seconds; the strength coach gets the full reasoning underneath. It runs for football and for indoor basketball — one system, the whole athlete.

Scene 12
MicroPulse — readiness, load, performance and return-to-play, turned into one clear, explainable decision per player, every day. The whole athlete, on one page.
