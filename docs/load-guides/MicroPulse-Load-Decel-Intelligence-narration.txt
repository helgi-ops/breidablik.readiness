Scene 1
Decel Intelligence. The cost of hard stops.

Scene 2
Deceleration — the cost of hard stops and cuts — predicts load on the knee and quadriceps better than running volume does. Here each player is assessed across five dimensions from the McBurnie et al. framework.

Scene 3
Here's the Decel Intelligence screen. Everything on it follows the same layered read: a plain verdict on top, the reason below, and the detail only if you open it.

Scene 4
How to read it. The chips OVERLOAD, UNDERLOAD and A:D are the dimensions; each flagged player gets the reason spelled out — that his recent braking exposure is under half of match-day demand, for instance. The citation sits beside the heading and confidence is given as the share of players with data.

Scene 5
When to open it. Open it to manage braking load — especially for players returning to play or with a quad or knee history. The research behind it: McBurnie, Harper, Jones & Dos'Santos (2022), Sports Medicine. Requires Pro.
