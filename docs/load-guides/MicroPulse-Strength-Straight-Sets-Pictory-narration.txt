Scene 3
Straight sets are exactly what they sound like: one exercise at a time, all its sets finished before you move on. Order the session with the main strength lift first — heavier, lower reps — then your push and pull work at a moderate load, and core or a carry to finish. Rest two to three minutes between heavy sets and about a minute to ninety seconds on accessories. Keep one or two reps in reserve on the main lifts so quality stays high.

Scene 4
The one thing to remember. One lift, all its sets, then the next. Heavy first, leave 1–2 in the tank.
