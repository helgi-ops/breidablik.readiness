A

Group into 3 dropdown menus

Keep the 3 most-used daily links flat (Dashboard · Players · Messages), then collapse the rest into two logical dropdown groups — Operations for workflow items, and Admin for settings, reporting, and integrations. Reduces visible items from 12 to 5.

B

Primary links + "More" overflow button

Show only the 5 most-used links flat in the nav, and move everything else into a single More ··· popover. The active page is highlighted with a filled pill so location is always clear. This is the simplest change — minimal visual disruption, works immediately.

C

Move to a left sidebar

Replace the top nav bar entirely with a collapsible left sidebar, grouped by category. This is the pattern from the reference image you shared earlier (Day At app). It scales to any number of links without crowding, shows the active section clearly, and frees up the full width of the page for content.
