Current state — what stands out

The player dashboard has three good building blocks (header, tabs, content) but a few things make it harder to use than it could be. The technical status badges in the header are noisy and don't lead with the most important signal. The tab bar is very large and chunky. The "Ready to Train" summary card appears on every tab, wasting space. And on the Dashboard tab, the wellbeing metrics (Fatigue: 3, Sleep: 5) are bare numbers with no visual context — a player can't immediately tell if their score is good or bad.

Lead with the readiness signal — redesign the header

The most important thing a player needs to know when they open the dashboard is their readiness status. Right now, YELLOW is the last badge in a row of 6 technical tags, easy to miss. Move the readiness status to a prominent pill on the right with a colour-tinted header background, and collapse the technical details (MD-3, NEURAL_VELOCITY, etc.) into a smaller secondary row. The player immediately knows their status without scanning.

B
Add visual context to the Dashboard metrics

On the Dashboard tab, wellbeing and load metrics are displayed as plain numbers (Fatigue: 3, Sleep: 5). A player has no way to tell at a glance if 3/10 is concerning or normal. Adding a thin coloured bar and a subtle background tint (green = good, amber = monitor, red = flag) lets the player read their status instantly, without needing to interpret raw values.

C
Make "Today" a dark hero strip — and slim the tab bar

The "Today" status message (Ready to Train / Modified Training) repeats on every tab as a plain white card. It's important information but gets lost. Moving it above the tabs as a fixed dark hero strip gives it presence and frees up tab content to focus on its own information. At the same time, swapping the big black pill tabs for a lighter underline style (like on the coach dashboard) reduces visual weight and gives more room to content.
